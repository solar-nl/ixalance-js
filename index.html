iXalance — The Black Lotus, 1998

idle

The original DOS binary runs instruction by instruction. A 64K intro generates all of its graphics procedurally before it draws anything, so Jizz shows a decrunch bar for a minute or two first — that is the intro working, not the page hanging. The music is generated by the demo itself and handed over part-way through, so it starts when the decrunch finishes.

With sound on, the demo follows the music, exactly as it did under the original loader — so where execution cannot keep up, the visuals skip rather than the audio stalling. Turn sound off for a smooth but silent run.
